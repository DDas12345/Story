10. Inspector Freddy (The Bureau's Resident Panic Expert)



  • Character Name: Inspector Fredericks (Freddy)
  • Age: 48 years old
  • Actor Name: Dinesh Phadnis
  • Character Overview:
    • Serious Fact: While Freddy may not be part of the official newer broadcast season lineup in real-world continuity, in my alternate universe he is fully alive, kicking, and serving as a veteran, loyal officer of the CID bureau who panics instantly at every spooky case.
    • Humorous Twist: Freddy spends 90% of his working hours utterly convinced that every locked-room murder involves ghosts or aliens, only to faint whenever Ruhi’s high-tech gadgets or Tittu the 129.3-kg robot cat accidentally drop from the ceiling during briefings.

 

11. Inspector Pankaj (The Professional Glutton)



  • Character Name: Sub-Inspector Pankaj
  • Age: 45 years old
  • Actor Name: Ajay Nagrath
  • Character Overview:
    • Serious Fact: Pankaj is a dedicated junior officer of the bureau who assists on major investigations, though his investigative focus is usually secondary to his appetite.
    • Humorous Twist: He is the mastermind behind the infamous "sweet heists," acting as an unofficial bribe-taker who uses young Ruhi and Mihika as accomplices to steal holy prashad and courier sweets, invariably getting caught with kalakand crumbs on his mustache and silver varq on his fingers.

 

12. Inspector Sachin (The Cool, Collected Sleuth)

  • Age: 50 years old
  • Actor Name: Hrishikesh Pandey

15. Dr. Salunkhe (The Legendary Forensics Maestro)



  • Character Name: Dr. Salunkhe
  • Actor Name: Narendra Gupta
  • Age: 65 years old
  • Character Overview:
    • Serious Fact: The undisputed maestro of forensics, Dr. Salunkhe can solve any murder mystery down to the exact microgram of dust, taking immense pride in his laboratory's absolute precision.
    • Humorous Twist: Hilariously egotistic and fiercely protective of his lab, he is the only person in the entire bureau with the absolute audacity to talk back to ACP Pradyuman during arguments—though his scientific sanity is constantly tested when Ruhi's stray gadget components contaminate his crime scene samples
