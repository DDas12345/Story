A few days later, Senior Inspector Abhijeet finally returned to Mumbai after his trip. Walking through the heavy glass doors of the bureau, he looked relaxed—right up until reality caught up with him. Within five minutes of stepping inside, his face was already covered in fresh, colorful bruises.

ACP Pradyuman stood right before him, his eyes flashing with absolute fury as he launched into a fiery scolding. "Besharam insaan! Shubh din par waisi gandi zubaan use karta hai, aur upar se apne ghar ke basement mein yeh saari ashleel kism ki cheezein aur items bhar kar rakhe hain tune! Tujhe sharam nahi aati?"

Fixing him with a piercing glare, ACP Pradyuman held out his hand. "Chal, abhi ke abhi mujhe us basement ke saare legal papers nikal kar dikha. Mujhe dekhna hai ki tera yeh kaunsa naya kissa chal raha hai!"

Abhijeet winced, rubbing his sore jaw. Under his breath, he muttered a sharp curse, glaring at the boss and calling him by his secret nickname: "Saala yeh Buddha Ravan... hamesha taang adata rehta hai, ek second ke liye bhi chain se nahi jeene deta, hamesha har cheez mein ghuste rehta hai!"

ACP Pradyuman narrowed his eyes, freezing mid-motion. "Ek minute... tune abhi kya kaha? Kuch kaha kya tune?"

Abhijeet’s eyes went wide with panic. He forced an awkward, innocent smile and stammered, "Nahi sir! Main toh bas... woh legal paper apne phone mein search kar raha hoon, kasam se!"

With trembling fingers, Abhijeet unlocked his phone, frantically scrolling through his files until he pulled up the digital property document. He shoved the screen forward. "Dekhiye sir! Yeh rahe saare legal papers, ekdum pakke aur asli hain!"

ACP Pradyuman snatched the phone, examining the document line by line. After a tense, agonizing moment of inspection, he handed the phone back with a huff. "Theek hai... kagaz toh poori tarah se valid hain. Bach gaya tu aaj!"

Relieved, Abhijeet quickly pocketed his phone, but the righteous indignation bubbled right back up inside him. Turning to ACP Pradyuman with an aggrieved expression, he whined, "Sir, ek baat bataiye... aap hamesha humaari personal life mein itna kyun interfere karte hain? Meri aur Daya ki zindagi mein taang adana jaise aapka full-time job ban gaya hai!"

Hearing this, Senior Inspector Daya—still sporting his own lingering bruises from earlier—walked over, nodding vigorously in complete agreement. "Haan sir, bilkul sahi keh raha hai Abhijeet! Aap thoda toh privacy diya kariye na, hum bhi mature log hain!"

Before either of them could blink, ACP Pradyuman’s expression turned utterly lethal. With terrifying speed, he swung his arm and landed a thunderous, open-handed slap straight across Abhijeet’s face, immediately followed by an identical backhand slap to Daya.

The impact was so forceful that both senior inspectors spun around in mid-air like tops before stumbling sideways into the filing cabinets.

ACP Pradyuman roared, pointing a furious finger right at them: "Haramkhoron! Zyaada zubaan mat chalao—baap hoon main tum dono ka!"

Abhijeet and Daya rubbed their spinning heads, too dizzy and terrified to say another word.
