The bureau bullpen was uncharacteristically quiet, save for the suspicious sound of enthusiastic chewing.

Avni, Pankaj, Ruhi, and Mihika were huddled together like professional criminals sharing a stash. Pankaj grinned broadly, waving his hand over the open containers.

"Khao, khao! Aur khao!" Pankaj urged, encouraging Avni, Ruhi, and Mihika to eat more mithai. "Itni saari mithai aayi hai, kisi ko pata nahi chalega!"

Suddenly, the heavy glass doors swung open, and Senior Inspector Daya and Inspector Purvi entered. Seeing her father walk in, little Mihika bolted across the room. She grabbed a piece of kaju katli, popped it straight into Daya's mouth, and handed another piece to Purvi.

Daya chewed, his eyes widening in pure bliss. "Waah! Mazaa aa gaya! Ekdum lajawab kaju katli hai!"

With terrifying speed, the six of them had finished every single box of the sweets.

A few seconds later, a loud, synchronized sound echoed through the room. Daya and Pankaj began burping loudly—HIC! BRPP!—causing Purvi and Avni to cover their noses and turn away in disgust.

Wiping his mouth, Daya glared at Pankaj and demanded, "Oye Pankaj! Yeh mithai tere chacha ki shadi ki thi kya jo aise thoonas raha hai?"

Pankaj shook his head innocently, burping again. "Nahi sir, kasam se mujhe kya pata kiska hai!"

Just then, Avni's eyes drifted down to a crumpled delivery slip stuck to the bottom of the empty cardboard box. She gasped, turning pale. "Mar gaye!"

Everyone froze. Ruhi and Mihika stopped chewing.

"Kya hua Avni? Aise kyon chilla rahi hai?" Purvi asked, stepping closer.

Avni held up the paper with trembling hands. "Ma'am, yeh... yeh box ACP sir ke liye tha! Special order kiya tha unhone!"

The entire group went dead silent. The realization hit them like a speeding truck: they had just devoured ACP Pradyuman's sacred, highly anticipated sweets.

Pankaj clutched his chest dramatically, collapsing onto a chair. "Hai Bhagwan! Main toh barbaad ho gaya!" he wailed, wishing aloud that the sweets had been poisoned so he could have died peacefully before ACP Pradyuman caught him.

Purvi crossed her arms, adding fuel to his anxiety. "Marna toh tu waise bhi hai, Pankaj. Tujhe pata nahi kya ki ACP sir apni is mithai ke liye kitne kanjoos hain? Ek ek tukda gin kar rakhte hain woh!"

Mihika giggled, piping up, "Isi liye toh Dadu ka blood pressure aur diabetes hamesha high rehte hain!"

Daya immediately reached out and lightly smacked Mihika on the head. "Oye, chup kar! Budhape mein pitwayegi kya mujhe?" he scolded her, though he was sweating bullets himself.

Avni bit her lip, desperately trying to think of a rescue plan. "Ek kaam karte hain, Abhijeet sir se bol kar Prayagraj se koi doosri mithai mangwa lete hain! Woh bhi toh wahan Maha Mahakhumbh mein gaye hue hain na?"

Daya shook his head frantically. "Dimag ka dahi ho gaya hai kya tera, Avni? Prayagraj se Mumbai delivery aane mein do-teen din lagenge! Tab tak toh ACP sir humara kimaa bana chuke honge!"

Avni turned her gaze toward the corner of the room, narrowing her eyes. "Waise Ruhi... tu apne papa Abhijeet sir ke paas Prayagraj kyun nahi gayi? Wahan aaram se ghoomti."

Purvi nodded in agreement, staring at the young gadgeteer. "Haan Ruhi, baat toh sahi hai. Tu toh hamesha apne papa ke saath chipki rehti hai."

Embarrassed, Ruhi—addressing Avni as "Didi" and Purvi as "Aunty"—scratched her head nervously. She leaned in close, whispering quietly in their ears, "Woh... mera personal problem hai, main period pain aur cramps se suffer kar rahi hoon, isliye nahi gayi."

Avni and Purvi blinked, their expressions settling into deadpan looks. "Oh."

Meanwhile, Daya, Pankaj, and Mihika stood completely clueless, watching the women whisper. Daya whispered to Pankaj, "Yeh aurton ke rahasya bhi na... bhagwan hi jaane kya baatein chal rahi hain."

Suddenly, the unmistakable sound of heavy, rhythmic footsteps echoed down the corridor.

Thud. Thud. Thud.

Panic struck. In a burst of pure survival instinct, the six of them frantically scooped up the empty wrappers and comically hid the sweet box behind a stack of old case files, pretending to look deeply invested in routine police work.

ACP Pradyuman (age 76) walked into the room, his sharp eyes scanning the area. "Ek minute... tum sab yahan kya kar rahe ho?" he asked, his voice booming. He crossed his arms. "Tum mein se kisi ne koi delivery item dekha hai kya? Mere ek dost ne Prayagraj se mere liye special mithai bheji thi. Mere phone par notification aa gaya hai ki deliver ho gayi hai, par box gayab hai!"

Ruhi and Pankaj immediately tried to deflect, talking over each other. "Arre ACP sir! Woh delivery boy hi kharab hoga!" Ruhi stammered. "Jhoothi entry kar di hogi usne! Woh toh kabhi time par kuch deliver karta hi nahi hai!"

Pankaj nodded vigorously, sweating profusely. "Haan sir! Bilkul nakli delivery boy hoga! Humne toh kuch nahi dekha!"

ACP Pradyuman narrowed his eyes, a dangerous, knowing smirk spreading across his face. He took a slow step forward. "Achha? Sach mein?" He looked straight at Pankaj. "Pankaj... waise tujhe kalakand bahut pasand hai na?"

Pankaj beamed automatically, nodding. "Haan sir, ekdum mooh mein ghul jaata hai—" He caught himself and slammed his mouth shut.

Ruhi and Mihika immediately face-palmed together. Idiot, their expressions screamed.

ACP Pradyuman turned his intense gaze toward Daya. "Aur Daya... kaju katli toh teri sabse pasandida mithai hai, hai na?"

Daya began sweating like he was sitting inside a sauna. "Nahi sir... woh toh main bas... fitness ke liye..."

With lightning speed, ACP Pradyuman pointed an accusing finger right at Daya's face. "Arey gadhon ki aulaad!" he roared. A shiny piece of silver vark from the kaju katli was stuck right to the tip of Daya's index finger.

Before Daya could react, Pradyuman spun around to face Pankaj. "O Mister Pankaj! Paav kilo ka kalakand toh teri moonchhon par atka hua hai! Pata nahi kitne kilo ka kalakand tere pet mein chala gaya hoga, besharam!"

Ruhi and Mihika burst out laughing uncontrollably, highly amused that ACP Pradyuman had indirectly called Pankaj fat.

Then, Pradyuman's eyes landed on his two granddaughters. He noticed sticky white chashni dripping down the sides of Ruhi and Mihika's faces from rasgullas and gulab jamuns.

Pradyuman closed his eyes in pure despair and cursed aloud, "Ram Ram Ram! In dono gadhon aur haramkhoron— Abhijeet aur Daya—ne kaisi aulaadein paali hain?!"

Offended that Pradyuman was dragging their parenting skills into the mix, Daya bristled up. "Arre sir, aap humari parenting par kyun—" Daya started, but a single, laser-focused glare from ACP Pradyuman made Daya instantly shrink back down into silence.

Both Ruhi and Mihika broke into classic anime-style sweatdrops, offering nervous, sheepish grins.

ACP Pradyuman turned his face away from them in deep disappointment, issuing an ultimatum. "Ab suno. Tum mein se jisne bhi woh box khola hai, woh seedha aage aaye!"

True to Indian custom, the moment the patriarch demanded someone step forward, everyone else instinctively took two steps back to avoid facing the consequences.

When ACP Pradyuman turned back around, only Pankaj was left standing isolated at the very front.

Pradyuman raised an eyebrow. "Toh matlab tune hi khola box?"

Pankaj's eyes went wide with panic. "Nahi sir! Maine kasam se haath bhi nahi lagaya!"

"Toh agar tune nahi khola, toh tu aage aake kyun khada hai?" Pradyuman snapped.

Pankaj looked around in confusion, realized everyone had abandoned him, and quickly tried to scurry backward. But Daya and Purvi immediately pointed fingers at him. "Sir, yahi hai asli mujrim! Isi ne khola tha!" Avni, Ruhi, and Mihika eagerly nodded in agreement to save their own skins.

ACP Pradyuman pointed a stern finger at Pankaj. "Chal, ab saza bhugt! Shuru ho ja—utthak-bethakh kar jaldi!"

Pankaj whimpered, instantly holding his ears and dropping into a squatting position, beginning his punishment while muttering prayers under his breath.

ACP Pradyuman could have beaten Pankaj with his famous belt, but he held back. Beating Pankaj made no sense; after all, Pankaj was just acting like a harmless, foolish kid giving in to his cravings.

However, Daya was a different story entirely. Daya deserved strict pitaai for failing to stop his daughter and actively corrupting her into a sweet-heist accomplice.

Five minutes later, Pankaj finished his squats, and Daya emerged from a brief "parental correction session" with ACP Pradyuman. Daya's face was covered with hilarious bruises, a swollen cheek, and ruffled hair.

As he limped back into the bullpen, Daya rubbed his sore arm and muttered under his breath, cursing the boss. "Saala budhau... hamesha satyanash karne aa jata hai... Buddha Ravan kahin ka!"

It was a well-known secret across the bureau: Abhijeet and Daya had secretly invented this exact codename for ACP Pradyuman behind his back—Buddha Ravan— whenever his anger crossed all limits.
