Time has passed, and the Pokemon are starting to feel very tired that they fall into a deep sleep.

The next day, the sun begins to rise up into the sky.

When all the Pokemon wake up, they soon find themselves in the Pokemon Center, much to their shock.

They all soon hurry out to see if the Snorlax Snowman is still there. Pikachu falls back, but everyone continues.

When they arrive, they see the Snorlax Snowman is still at the same spot. They are glad to see it back, but they have to question… was this all a dream or did it really happen?
