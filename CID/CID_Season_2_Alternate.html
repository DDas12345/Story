CID Season 2: Alternate Universe Crossover Madness

by Devcreation

Summary

Dive into a hilarious alternate universe where the gritty world of CID collides with high-tech gadgets, blue cat robots, and chaotic cross-franchise madness.

Chapter 1: CID: Alternate Universe - Cast &amp; Character Profiles (Part 1)

I am writing the character overview for my story.

1. Ruhi (MY OC and Abhijeet's Older Daughter & Master Gadgeteer)

  • Character Name: Ruhi

  • Actor Name: Parineeti Chopra (channeling her eccentric, brilliant, and caffeinated persona as Meeta from Hasee Toh Phasee)

  • Age: 20 years old

  • Character Overview:

    • Serious Fact: As Abhijeet's older adopted daughter— rescued as a newborn from a dangerous underworld syndicate— Ruhi possesses a genius-level intellect for experimental engineering, quantum physics, and futuristic gadget creation. 

    • Humorous Twist: Operating entirely on a chaotic mix of intense focus, random late-night blueprints, and zero regard for standard safety protocols, she treats the household like her personal laboratory.

(A/N: Please note that Doraemon does not exist in this world. Instead, all iconic futuristic gadgets, names, features, and inventions you know are engineered entirely by our brilliant master gadgeteer, Ruhi.)

2. Tittu (The 129.3-kg Robotic Cat Companion)



  • Character Name: Tittu

  • Actor/Model Inspiration: Classic 22nd-century blue robotic cat design with a modern alternate universe twist

  • Weight & Specs: 129.3 kilograms of pure emotional intelligence, equipped with Ruhi's customized 4D Pocket on his white belly

  • Character Overview:

    • Serious Fact: Tittu is Ruhi's ultimate technological masterpiece— a sentient, highly sophisticated robotic cat loaded with futuristic gadgets, quantum storage fields, and deep loyalty to his creators.

    • Humorous Twist: Despite being a high-tech machine, he suffers from a debilitating phobia of mice (screaming "Chuhaaaaaaaaaaaaaaa!" and flattening anyone nearby), boasts a tragic origin involving lost ears due to a toilet plunger mishap and a yellow-to-blue paint corrosion from crying over Liquid Sorrow, and spends his nights ruling the upper bunk of Ruhi's room while hoarding an empire of sweet Japanese dorayaki wrappers.

(A/N: Just to clarify—while the character of Doraemon does not exist in this alternate universe, his iconic physical appearance, blue paint, and high-tech features most certainly do)

3. ACP Pradyuman



  • Character Name: ACP Pradyuman

  • Actor Name: Shivaji Satam

  • Age: 76 years old

  • Character Overview:

    • Serious Fact: As the legendary head of the CID bureau, he is an absolute master of deduction, famously known for his intense glare, dramatic hand gestures ("Daya, darwaza tod do!"), and decades of unwavering dedication to justice.

    • Humorous Twist: In this alternate universe, his primary full-time job isn't even solving murder cases anymore— it's managing the daily heart attacks caused by Abhijeet and Daya's pervert nature, keeping a strict belt handy for emergency parental discipline, and dealing with Ruhi's reality-bending gadgets turning the bureau upside down.

4. Senior Inspector Abhijeet




  • Character Name: Senior Inspector Abhijeet

  • Actor Name: Aditya Srivastava

  • Age: 57 years old (making him one year younger than Daya in my AU)

  • Character Overview:

    • Serious Fact: Abhijeet is one of the most brilliant, sharp-minded investigators in the bureau, fiercely protective of his team and deeply emotional when it comes to his family— most notably his older gadgeteer daughter Ruhi (whom he rescued as a newborn from an international smuggling syndicate) and his sensitive, mute younger daughter Shreya.

    • Humorous Twist: Despite being a senior cop, he is an absolute disaster of a secret agent at home. Between secretly flirting with Dr. Tarika, filling his bungalow's basement with questionable gadgets, and trying to act like a strict father while his 130-kg cat robot Tittu destroys his living room, he spends half his life dodging ACP Pradyuman's wrath.

5. Senior Inspector Daya



  • Character Name: Senior Inspector Daya

  • Actor Name: Dayanand Shetty

  • Age: 58 years old (one year older than Abhijeet in my AU)

  • Character Overview:

    • Serious Fact: Known as the powerhouse of CID with legendary physical strength, Daya can break down any door in India with a single kick and is Abhijeet's ride-or-die partner through thick and thin.

    • Humorous Twist: While he is terrifying to criminals, he is completely helpless against his daughter Mihika (the "Desi Himawari") and ends up taking the physical brunt of ACP Pradyuman's belt whenever their joint sweet-heists and parenting blunders get exposed.

6. Mihika (The "Desi Himawari" of the Household)



    • Character Name: Mihika

    • Actor Name: [Child Actor / Unspecified]

    • Relationship: Biological daughter of Daya and Shreya

    • Character Overview:

      • Serious Fact: Growing up in a household full of elite CID officers, Mihika brings immense joy, warmth, and innocence to the family, safely far away from any dark underworld conspiracies or kidnappings.

      • Humorous Twist: Known universally as the "Desi Himawari" of the family, she possesses an uncanny knack for absolute chaos. Whether she is weaponizing innocent questions to make her mother Shreya's ears burn, demanding high-speed Bullet motorcycle rides from Daya, or treating Ruhi's experimental laboratory like a playground, she keeps every single inspector— and ACP Pradyuman's backup belt— permanently on high alert.

7. The mute Shreya (Abhijeet's Younger Daughter)



  • Character Name: Shreya

  • Actor Name: Sparsh Khanchandani (portraying her as the younger child in the household)

  • Age: 17

  • Character Overview:

    • Serious Fact: Shreya is Abhijeet's sweet, incredibly sensitive adopted daughter who is mute, communicating seamlessly through expressive gestures, facial expressions, and written notes. She brings a deep emotional warmth to the household and serves as the calm, innocent counter-balance to Ruhi's chaotic gadget experiments.

    • Humorous Twist: Whenever Tittu accidentally triggers a gravity-inversion field or Ruhi's lie-detector flower starts exposing everyone's secrets, poor Shreya is usually found standing nearby watching the domestic circus with wide, bewildered eyes, holding up a written sign wondering why her family can't have a normal day without explosions.

 

8. Dr. Tarika (The Brilliant Forensic Expert & Abhijeet's wife)



  • Character Name: Dr. Tarika

  • Actor Name: Shraddha Musale

  • Age: 42

  • Character Overview:

    • Serious Fact: As the premier forensic expert of the CID bureau, Dr. Tarika is exceptionally sharp, methodical, and dedicated to uncovering scientific evidence that cracks the most complex cases wide open. At home, she is Abhijeet's loving wife and the devoted legal mother to Ruhi and Shreya.

    • Humorous Twist: While she is an expert at handling high-tech forensic equipment at work, her primary challenge at home is managing the sheer chaos of her household— from dealing with Abhijeet's shameless flirting to confiscating Ruhi's reality-bending gadgets before they accidentally turn the living room inside out.

9. CID Officer Shreya (The Fearless Investigator & Daya's Wife)



  • Character Name: CID Officer Shreya

  • Actor Name: Janvi Chheda

  • Age: 42

  • Character Overview:

    • Serious Fact: As a dedicated and sharp-witted officer of the CID bureau, Shreya handles dangerous criminals and high-stakes investigations with absolute grace and fearlessness. In this alternate universe, she is happily married to Daya, and they are the proud parents of their biological daughter, Mihika.

    • Humorous Twist: While she is a terror to criminals on duty, her real test begins at home—where she has to constantly rescue Daya from ACP Pradyuman's wrath, stop Mihika from unleashing "Desi Himawari" chaos across the house, and figure out how to disable Ruhi's latest experimental gadget before it swallows the living room furniture.

Chapter 2: CID: Alternate Universe - Cast &amp; Character Profiles (Part 2)

Chapter Summary

The remaining characters of my story.

10. Inspector Freddy (The Bureau's Resident Panic Expert)



  • Character Name: Inspector Fredericks (Freddy)
  • Age: 48 years old
  • Actor Name: Dinesh Phadnis
  • Character Overview:
    • Serious Fact: While Freddy may not be part of the official newer broadcast season lineup in real-world continuity, in my alternate universe he is fully alive, kicking, and serving as a veteran, loyal officer of the CID bureau who panics instantly at every spooky case.
    • Humorous Twist: Freddy spends 90% of his working hours utterly convinced that every locked-room murder involves ghosts or aliens, only to faint whenever Ruhi’s high-tech gadgets or Tittu the 129.3-kg robot cat accidentally drop from the ceiling during briefings.

 

11. Inspector Pankaj (The Professional Glutton)



  • Character Name: Sub-Inspector Pankaj
  • Age: 45 years old
  • Actor Name: Ajay Nagrath
  • Character Overview:
    • Serious Fact: Pankaj is a dedicated junior officer of the bureau who assists on major investigations, though his investigative focus is usually secondary to his appetite.
    • Humorous Twist: He is the mastermind behind the infamous "sweet heists," acting as an unofficial bribe-taker who uses young Ruhi and Mihika as accomplices to steal holy prashad and courier sweets, invariably getting caught with kalakand crumbs on his mustache and silver varq on his fingers.

 

12. Inspector Sachin (The Cool, Collected Sleuth)



  • Character Name: Inspector Sachin
  • Age: 50 years old
  • Actor Name: Hrishikesh Pandey
  • Character Overview:
    • Serious Fact: Sachin is a sharp, street-smart, and reliable inspector known for his cool head during high-adrenaline shootouts and complex field operations.
    • Humorous Twist: He tries his absolute best to stay entirely out of Abhijeet and Daya's domestic scandals, but always ends up getting dragged into the chaos whenever stray gadget blasts or flying furniture cross the bullpen.

 

13. Sub-Inspector Avni (The Sharp New-Gen Officer)



  • Character Name: Sub-Inspector Avni
  • Age: 24 years old
  • Actor Name: Saumya Saraswat
  • Character Overview:
    • Serious Fact: Avni is an energetic and modern addition to the CID team, bringing sharp tactical skills and modern tech-savviness to contemporary cases.
    • Humorous Twist: Having joined the bureau recently, she is still learning that "standard police procedure" in this alternate universe includes dodging gravity-inversion fields, negotiating with a robotic blue cat, and hiding snacks from ACP Pradyuman's eagle eyes.

 

14. Inspector Purvi (The Fearless Field Operative)



  • Character Name: Inspector Purvi
  • Age: 40 years old
  • Actor Name: Ansha Sayed
  • Character Overview:
    • Serious Fact: Purvi is a fierce, highly capable officer who handles dangerous criminals with martial arts proficiency and cool tactical precision.
    • Humorous Twist: She frequently acts as the designated emergency catch-net for people fainting—most notably saving Ruhi from micro-heart-attacks whenever Abhijeet starts publicly flirting with Dr. Tarika.

 

15. Dr. Salunkhe (The Legendary Forensics Maestro)



  • Character Name: Dr. Salunkhe
  • Actor Name: Narendra Gupta
  • Age: 65 years old
  • Character Overview:
    • Serious Fact: The undisputed maestro of forensics, Dr. Salunkhe can solve any murder mystery down to the exact microgram of dust, taking immense pride in his laboratory's absolute precision.
    • Humorous Twist: Hilariously egotistic and fiercely protective of his lab, he is the only person in the entire bureau with the absolute audacity to talk back to ACP Pradyuman during arguments—though his scientific sanity is constantly tested when Ruhi's stray gadget components contaminate his crime scene samples

     

 

16. Dr. Gargi (The Sharp Forensic Assistant)



  • Character Name: Dr. Gargi
  • Age: 31 years old
  • Actor Name: Parul Rai
  • Character Overview:
    • Serious Fact: As a vital part of the forensic team, Dr. Gargi provides crucial medical and chemical analysis to support Dr. Salunkhe during complex medico-legal autopsies.
    • Humorous Twist: She is often the one holding the microscope while Dr. Salunkhe dramatically questions the laws of physics after discovering that a suspect's hair fiber was somehow manufactured in the 22nd century.
